How responses are saved

Progress is backed up locally in this browser as you click. At the end, the study starts JSON and CSV downloads and shows buttons to download either file again.

Allow multiple downloads if your browser asks. A browser backup is not a shared or cloud dataset.
Manual pacing is locked on: story and rating pages never move forward on their own. Use the exact same participant ID to reproduce the same condition, order, sides, colors, and item randomization.
