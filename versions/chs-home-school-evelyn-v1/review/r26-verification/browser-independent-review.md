# Independent screenshot review

## Runs 01–06

Reviewed all 72 saved choice screenshots independently using six contact sheets. These runs cover the woman-role HUG conditions, all four profiles in Home → School and the first two profiles in School → Home.

No new visible caption/foreground rendering defects were found in these 72 captured choice states:

- Dark top captions, role labels, and bottom options render correctly on yellow, orange, light green, and magenta pages. No missing, doubled, or visibly clipped letters were seen.
- White eyes and existing character hues remain intact; no dark recoloring spills into characters or background artwork.
- All role labels and option phrases agree visually with the displayed left/right characters, including the reversed profiles.
- School and home palette accents appear consistent with their associated character sets at the reviewed display size.
- Highlight borders are visible in front of the scene/caption boundary and do not obscure caption words in the captured frames.
- All scenes appear aligned and equally sized. The saved measurements independently confirm identical 716.797 × 403.195 bounds across all 576 pages from these six runs, with zero detected caption overflows.

The saved metadata sometimes includes the transient “Loading the sound… Please wait.” indicator. That alone is not evidence of a failure or a long delay; the saved screenshots/metadata do not provide audio-start timing or demonstrate whether the word “At” is audible. Natural playback/audio quality requires the separate listening and timing checks.

Limitations: this is a review of saved local-browser choice screenshots, not a new hosted CHS session. It does not inspect every transient animation frame, every intrinsic-resolution background pixel, exterior/hallway details, or audio. It does not yet cover later runs. Original screenshots are retained in `browser/`; derived contact sheets are in `browser-contact-sheets/`.
